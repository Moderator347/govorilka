Chapter 1. A Wonderful Morning

What a wonderful day! The sun is shining, and I am so happy, absolutely thrilled! We celebrate, we laugh, everything is amazing and beautiful.

She sat alone by the cold window and wept. The farewell had taken everything: her love, her hope, her dear friend. Now only sorrow and loneliness remained, and the gloom would never lift...

STOP! COWARD! Fight, you brave warrior, fight! Stand strong, defend the honor, never run away from danger! The battle is won, victory is ours!

The shadow moved silently in the dark corridor... Something strange and mysterious waited behind the fog. Who is there? A whisper, an eerie silence, a creepy unknown.
